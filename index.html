Who We Are

Rosette Robotics is a technology company focused on developing robotics solutions for agricultural and seed analysis applications. Our mission is to improve efficiency, consistency, and data accuracy through intelligent, analyst-centred semi-automation.

Our Product

Our flagship robotic platform, the Rosette SABER, semi-automates seed inspection and analysis using advanced imaging, machine learning, and robotics coupled with human review intended to blend machine and human analyst intelligence. The system provides fast, reliable insights while reducing manual work, but not taking analysis out of the hands of expert analysts.
